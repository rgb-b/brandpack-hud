Weekly maintenance

Function	 ↳ Clean	 ↳ Manual Clean
Remove left-side cover
	• Remove 2 white screws at the top of the cover
Remove cutting rail
	• One white screw to remove.
Replace left-side cover
	• No need to re-do the screws, just sit the cover in place
Enter on console
Remove left-side cover again
Remove right-side cover
	• 2 White screws, identical to left side
Take solvent bottle from shelf and fill cap with solvent
Lay out a few layers of paper towel
Take the foam swabs and dip in solvent
	• Never dip the cleaning swab back into the solvent
Clean left-side print head 
	• Do not clean ON print head unless troubleshooting 
	• Use swab to clean dust/dirt/ink from gaps around print head
Clean right-side wiper and ink cap 
	• Do not clean ON ink cap
	• Use swab to clean area around ink cap, avoid damaging the rubber gasket.
	• Use swab to clean ink off wiper gently. Use more pressure if there is dried ink build up on the wiper.
	• Reach swab down into ink well underneath the wiper and soak up excess ink
Repeat until clean
Replace right-side cover
Replace left-side cover and press enter on console 
Remove left-side cover and replace cutter rail
Replace left-side cover
Disposing of cleaning swabs and liquid
Lay all cleaning swabs on paper towels (~3 paper towels, folded)
Roll up, and wrap in tape until fully covered
Dispose of in general waste

General maintenance/housekeeping
Preventative Service

Every 6 months, a Roland technician should be organised to service the proofer.
After the technician services the proofer, they should change the date on the sticker in the upper right corner to the next service date, in 6 months' time.
Before the service tech arrives, an Esko Gradation Strip should be ran on the Roland.
Gradation strip location:
Production Server
↳ Backstage Users
↳ John
↳ “*Esko gradation.tif*”
Print, laminate and mount the gradation strip
After the service has been completed, run the gradation strip again before the technician leaves, and compare with the earlier print.
Check for problems, defects, density, hue, and other changes.
Date both test strips and label as “Before service” and “After service”
